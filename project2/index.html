Trailblazer Competitive & Social Tennis

Connecting Utah Tech students across all academic majors through high-level tennis competition, team development, and camaraderie in St. George, Utah.

Utah Tech Tennis Club is a selective, competitive co-ed tennis team comprised of Utah Tech undergraduate and graduate students. Competing in the USTA Intermountain section and national invitationals, we test our game against universities across Utah, Nevada, Colorado, and the West Coast.

Beyond competition, what we take the greatest pride in is our tight-knit Trailblazer community. We connect tennis enthusiasts of varying backgrounds, providing organized practices, stroke mechanics coaching, cardio drills, and social activities across Southern Utah—from team dinners and Grand Slam watch parties to group hikes in Snow Canyon.

Whether you competed in high school varsity or are looking to hone your match play alongside fellow students, our club provides the court time, training, and competitive atmosphere you need to thrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers regarding club membership, team practices, tournament travel, dues, and court access.

How do I join the Utah Tech Tennis Club?
All enrolled Utah Tech University undergraduate and graduate students are welcome to join. To get started, join our official Instagram group chat, register on the campus recreation portal, and come out to our next practice session next to Innovation Plaza!
Where and when are practices held?
Practices are held three times a week at the Utah Tech Tennis Courts (Next to Innovation Plaza) in St. George: Mondays and Wednesdays from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM, and Fridays from 4:30 PM to 6:30 PM.
Are team practices mandatory?
Members are strongly encouraged to attend practices to develop their game and enjoy the team environment, but attendance is flexible around your academic class schedules. However, practice attendance and challenge match results are primary criteria used for USTA Tennis On Campus travel roster selection!
What are practices typically like?
Practices are structured and dynamic. A typical session opens with dynamic stretching and mini-tennis warm-ups, progresses into high-repetition feeding drills (cross-court consistency, approach shots, volleys), followed by live point-play simulations and competitive King of the Court sets.
How do I find a hitting partner outside of practice?
We connect all our players directly through our official Instagram group chat! Members frequently post their NTRP rating, preferred rally days, and court availability to coordinate casual hits and practice matches.
What tournaments does the club compete in?
We compete in the USTA Tennis On Campus Intermountain sectionals, regional collegiate invitationals (against BYU, Utah, USU, UNLV, and Northern Arizona), and strive for bids to the USTA Tennis On Campus National Championship in the spring.
How much are club team dues?
Club dues are kept affordable for students and are announced at the start of each semester. Dues help fund practice tennis balls, team match jerseys, tournament entry fees, and travel expenses. We work with Utah Tech Club Sports to ensure finances are never a barrier for active players.
Does Utah Tech Tennis Club offer private lessons?
The club does not offer paid private tennis lessons. However, club practices include peer coaching from our leadership team. For beginner instruction, students can enroll in university P.E. tennis courses or reach out through our community chat.

Utah Tech Tennis Club is an official student-run sports club of Utah Tech University, promoting competitive excellence, athletic development, and recreational play across Southern Utah.
